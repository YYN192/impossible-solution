TWO REAL STORMS

Earth can warn you about only one of them.

PARTICLE BURST · 20 MAY 2024

☢️

Arrives in tens of minutes

No warning from Earth can beat it. Curiosity measured 8.1 mGy on Mars from this one storm: about 39 normal days of radiation at once.

STORM CLOUD (CME) · 23 MAY 2024

🌩️

Takes days to reach Mars

NASA’s DONKI forecast said it would brush past Mars on 28 May. Earth can warn you, if you spent a worker to Ask Earth.

Ask Earth: “Your message takes about 12 minutes to reach Earth. Scientists study it overnight. Their answer is waiting for you in the morning.”

Sources: JPL / Curiosity RAD release, May 2024 · NASA DONKI WSA-Enlil

Two kinds of storm, both real. A particle burst arrives in tens of minutes, so no warning from Earth can beat it. On 20 May 2024 Curiosity measured 8.1 milligray from one storm, about 39 normal Mars days at once. A storm cloud takes days, so Earth can warn you, but only if you spent a worker to Ask Earth. Help from Earth can't beat a particle blast. (~25 s)
