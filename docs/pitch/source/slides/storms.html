Two kinds of storm, both real. A particle burst arrives in tens of minutes, so no warning from Earth can beat it. On 20 May 2024 Curiosity measured 8.1 milligray from one storm; at its worst hour the radiation was about 74 times the level just before it (NASA RadLab). A storm cloud takes days, so Earth can warn you, but only if you spent a worker to Ask Earth. DONKI forecast it would brush past Mars on 28 May. The game uses live DONKI data when online and a saved snapshot offline, always badged honestly. (~25 s)
