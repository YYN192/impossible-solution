THE PROBLEM · WHO IT IS FOR

Kids see what happens on Mars. They never see the choices behind it.

Rover photos and landing videos show the results. A real mission is a chain of trade-offs: power, air, people and time. Skip one job today, and you pay for it later. We made this for kids aged 7–12 and their classrooms, in English and Bulgarian.

The problem: kids see Mars outcomes, never the decisions. Photos show a rover on the surface, not the choices it took to keep it running. On a real base there is never enough power, never enough people, and Earth is minutes away. (~15 s)
