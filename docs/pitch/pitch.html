REAL STORM DATA · 20 MAY 2024

Earth can warn you about only one of them.

PARTICLE BURST · 20 MAY 2024

☢️

Arrives in tens of minutes

No warning from Earth can beat it. Curiosity measured 8.1 mGy on Mars from this one storm: about 39 normal days of radiation at once.

STORM CLOUD (CME) · 23 MAY 2024

🌩️

Takes days to reach Mars

NASA’s DONKI forecast said it would brush past Mars on 28 May. Earth can warn you, if you spent a worker to Ask Earth.

Curiosity RAD baseline: 0.21 mGy per day (Hassler et al., 2014). DONKI CME 2024-05-20T05:36Z is the storm on sol 2 of the main mission. A message to Earth takes 3–22 minutes one way.

Sources: JPL / Curiosity RAD release, May 2024 · NASA DONKI WSA-Enlil · Live DONKI data when online; saved snapshot offline, badged SAVED DATA, never called live

Two kinds of storm, both real. A particle burst arrives in tens of minutes, so no warning from Earth can beat it. On 20 May 2024 Curiosity measured 8.1 milligray from one storm, about 39 normal Mars days at once; the normal rate is 0.21 milligray per day. A storm cloud takes days, so Earth can warn you, but only if you spent a worker to Ask Earth. DONKI forecast it would brush past Mars on 28 May. The game uses live DONKI data when online and a saved snapshot offline, always badged honestly. (~25 s)

What this game simplifies

REAL NASA SCIENCE

  • The 20 May 2024 solar storm, measured on Mars
  • Normal radiation on the Mars surface
  • Dust cutting solar power by about a third
  • The 3–22 minute Earth–Mars message delay
  • DONKI’s storm-cloud forecast for Mars

GAME NUMBERS

  • 3 crew and 5 jobs (a real base has more)
  • Short sols: a days-long storm cloud fits in 1–2 sols
  • Job costs and the size of each bar
  • Space-ray meter units (the storm-vs-normal ratio is real)
  • Earth’s dust forecast

Inside the game, every number says what it is: real NASA number or game number. In class: an 8-minute mission, a teacher card with a lesson goal and 3 questions, no login, works offline, English and Bulgarian.
